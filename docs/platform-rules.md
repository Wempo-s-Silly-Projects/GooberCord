GooberCord — Platform Rules

GooberCord is an all-ages chat platform, made for players of every age. Anyone
can create an account and take part on equal, fair terms whatever their race,
ethnicity, ideology, nationality, religion, beliefs, sex, gender identity,
gender expression, sexual orientation, disability, or background. Anything
that disrupts that is not allowed here.

About these rules

These rules apply to all behavior on GooberCord: messages, DMs and group chats,
mentions, uploaded media, emoji and stickers, server names and settings, and
profile fields alike. Moderation on this platform works in two layers — server
owners and their moderators run the day-to-day rule inside their own servers
(AutoMod lanes, timeouts, bans, the verification gate), and the rules below are
the platform floor that applies everywhere, in every server and in every DM.
A server's own rules can be stricter; they can never be looser.

These rules may change, and we will announce added or changed rules.

The most important part of these rules is the idea behind them. Follow the
spirit of the rules, not just the word. If something feels wrong, or you feel
like you shouldn't be doing it, you probably shouldn't be doing it. Nothing is
allowed just because it isn't written down here — do not look for loopholes
in these rules; there are none. Ask a staff member if a rule is unclear.

The rules

1. Harassment and raids
Spreading rude remarks, mocking or targeting specific users or groups, sexual
harassment, evading a block or a ban, and coordinating or taking part in
server raids are prohibited. Do not tarnish the good will of our fellow
users. Report problems through the report feature or contact the moderators.

2. Threats
Threatening any person or group — directly, indirectly, or suggestively — with
violence or harm is prohibited, and includes threats exchanged in DMs.

3. Discrimination
Hate speech, and content that attacks, mocks, excludes, dehumanizes or
encourages hatred toward a person or group, is prohibited. That includes
discrimination keyed to race, ethnicity, nationality or country of origin,
religion or belief, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation,
disability, medical condition or neurodivergence, age, body type, political
belief, social or economic status. You may not promote hate groups,
extremist beliefs, segregation, exclusion, or the idea that any group of
people is inferior, unnatural, dangerous or unwelcome.

4. Illegal and dangerous content
All content must follow real-world law. Prohibited: drug references or
instructions, tobacco and vaping, alcohol use, terrorism and threats of
terrorist attacks, death threats, graphic or glorified discussion of
self-harm, suicide or eating disorders, extreme violence or gore, animal
cruelty, defamation, misleading content that carries real danger to people
or public safety, and the sale or facilitation of regulated or dangerous
goods. Sharing or selling cheats and hacks for games is also prohibited.

5. Child endangerment
GooberCord is for all ages, including children. Any content, activity, or
discussion that puts a child in a dangerous, uncomfortable, or sexual
situation results in immediate account termination as well as a report filed
to local authorities. Child safety outranks every other consideration here.

6. Sexual content — a total ban
No sexual content of any kind is allowed on GooberCord, anywhere. GooberCord
serves players under 18, so it does not offer age-restricted or 18+ spaces at
all: references to pornography, sex, exposed body parts, slang, suggestive
acronyms, anatomical terms, or any direct or indirect reference to sexual
behavior are prohibited and result in immediate account termination. This
covers text, images and video, avatars, banners, emoji, stickers, and
profiles, whether the subject is you, someone else, a drawn or fictional
figure, or an AI-generated image, and whether the media was shared with
consent or not. Do not report violations by vigilante means — report them and
leave the investigating to the moderators.

7. Personal information
Do not disclose information about yourself or other users that could put
someone at risk: addresses, real names (yours or a family member's), phone
numbers, e-mail addresses, ages or dates of birth, payment details, or
personal social media accounts. Do not share or threaten to share someone
else's private information without their consent.

8. Profanity
GooberCord does not allow profanity of any kind, in any language: cussing,
slander, hateful words, or references to hateful or sexualized topics. A chat
filter detects and blocks this language. Bypassing the filter — omitting
letters, adding spaces or characters, special characters, letter or emoji
substitutes, or any other way of carrying the word's meaning across — is not
allowed, and saying you were "just testing the filter" does not excuse it.
Profanity is equally barred from every user-creatable surface: posts, server
names, nicknames, emoji, and uploaded media.

9. Spam and mention flooding
Do not spam: repeated identical messages, walls of repeated or random
characters, massive bursts of content, or advertising unrelated third-party
services. Do not farm the mention system: mass-pinging members or groups,
sending unwanted repeated mentions to a person, or using @mentions to
harass, distract, or evade the rules. Attempts to bypass spam controls are
treated as a violation in their own right.

10. Honest accounts and servers
One account, one human. Do not share, buy, sell, transfer, or trade accounts,
login credentials, access codes, servers, or invite links; a group or clan
server account stays with a single owner. Do not create fake profiles or
impersonate a person, group, or organization. Do not inflate server
membership or engagement with fake accounts. Do not create or use other
accounts after removal for rule violations. Misleading reports and appeals
that waste staff action are prohibited too.

11. Media uploads
Uploaded images, videos, emoji, and stickers follow all the rules above: no
sexual content (rule 6), no violence, gore or animal cruelty (rule 4), no
profanity or filter bypass in filenames or embedded contents (rule 8), no
attacks on other users (rule 3), no malware. An upload that breaks the rules
is exactly as prohibited as the same content typed into a message, and the
upload ceiling follows the verified upload tiers on the parity board
(docs/parity-limits.md).

12. Gambling — including with virtual currency
Gambling of any kind is banned on GooberCord, on-platform or off-site,
whether it breaks real-world law or not. This includes risking Goober Coins
or any virtual item for a chance to win something of value, coin or item
sweepstakes, lotteries or raffles, pay-to-enter giveaways or contests, places
that charge virtual currency for a chance at a prize, and encouraging or
linking to third-party gambling services, including gambling with virtual
items. Deceptive schemes to take other users' coins or money are prohibited
under rule 10 as well.

13. Off-platform behavior
Our primary focus is on GooberCord, but we may take action on serious
behavior that happens off-platform when it poses a real risk to this
community: credible grooming or exploitation of minors, threats of violence,
stalking, raiding or doxxing of our users or staff, involvement in hate
groups or violent extremist movements, or documented abuse or criminal
behavior that could endanger users. Users removed for off-platform behavior
are generally not eligible to return. If someone endangers this community,
report it.

14. Security
Do not steal accounts or credentials, run phishing, spread malware, or attack
the service or its users with flooding or denial-of-service attempts.
